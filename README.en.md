<details>
<summary><b>📺 How do I show the game on my stream?</b></summary>

**TikTok LIVE Studio**
1. Open the game from the app.
2. In LIVE Studio press «Add source» and choose «Window capture».
3. Pick the game's window (its name starts with «M7»).

**OBS**
- Add a **Browser Source** with the game link the app shows you, or add a **Window Capture** of the game's window.

> ⌨️ **F11** or a double click on the game = full screen, and **Esc** brings it back.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🎨 How do I put an overlay on my stream?</b></summary>

1. Open the **«Overlays»** tab and pick a section, then a look.
2. Press **«📋 Copy link»**.
3. In LIVE Studio add a **«Link»** source, and in OBS add a **Browser Source** with the size written under the link, then paste the link.

> The overlay starts empty and fills up with support during your stream. If you change «Places» in the app, it changes on your stream right away.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🌐 How do I change the app's language?</b></summary>

Press the **«ع | E»** switch at the top of the app, or pick the language in **Settings**. The installer also asks you the first time, and it picks your PC's language for you.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🐪 How do I run Camel Race?</b></summary>

1. You need a **Stream Legend** subscription.
2. Open «Camel Race» from the app and press «Download» (once only, then it updates itself).
3. In the **game settings** choose the number of heats, the track length, the registration time and the quality.
4. Start the game and capture its window in LIVE Studio or OBS. Viewers join by typing «هجن» or with any gift.

> 📋 If someone asks which heat they're in, press the «Heat list» button in the app to show who is racing now.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🛡️ Windows says the file is unknown — what do I do?</b></summary>

That's normal with new programs. Press «More info» and then «Run anyway».
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🎁 The game doesn't receive gifts</b></summary>

1. Make sure you're live, and that the username is typed correctly without @.
2. Make sure the game that's open is the one selected in the app.
3. Try «Trial mode»: if it works, the problem is the connection to TikTok and it will come back shortly.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>⏳ My subscription ended in the middle of a stream — will it cut off?</b></summary>

No. The stream carries on normally until you disconnect, then the free plan comes back until you renew. If you renew before your subscription ends, the new time is added to what you have left.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🖥️ Can I run the games from another PC?</b></summary>

Yes, with Stream Knight and Stream Legend. In **Settings ← Second PC** turn the option on and open the link on the other PC (it must be on the same network). The app stays on the streaming PC, and the other PC shows the games in OBS.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💻 Does it need a powerful PC?</b></summary>

Most games are light and run inside the app, so any Windows 10 or 11 PC that can stream is enough.

The big games (**Camel Race**, **City of Silence**, **Knock 'Em Down!** and **Legends Arena**) run in their own window and download once from inside the app, and all of them except Legends Arena need a graphics card with DirectX 12. If your stream stutters, lower the quality in the game settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔄 How do I update the app?</b></summary>

The app updates itself. When a new version comes out you'll see a notice inside it — press «Update» and you're done.
